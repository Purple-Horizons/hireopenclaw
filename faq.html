Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is HireOpenClaw?

HireOpenClaw is a managed AI assistant service. We set up and run AI "employees" that handle tasks like content creation, social media, lead research, and admin work. You chat with them via Telegram or WhatsApp — no technical setup required.

Is this just ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT is a general-purpose chat window. Our AI employees are configured for your business — they know your tools, your voice, and your workflows. They're connected to your calendar, email, CRM, and social media. They don't just answer questions, they do work.

Who is this for?

Business owners and teams who want AI automation but don't have time (or patience) to set it up themselves. If you've ever tried to configure AI tools and gave up after 3 hours, this is for you.

Setup & Onboarding

How long does setup take?

24-48 hours from when you complete our intake form. We handle all the technical setup — API keys, integrations, configurations, testing. You just answer some questions about your business.

What do you need from me?

Basic info about your business, access to accounts you want connected (social media, calendar, etc.), and examples of your brand voice. Plus 15-20 minutes for a quick walkthrough call.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Just Telegram or WhatsApp on your phone (which you probably already have).

Pricing & Billing

How much does it cost?

Starter: $299/mo (1 AI employee) · Team: $799/mo (up to 3 AI employees) · Enterprise: Custom pricing (unlimited). All plans include dedicated infrastructure and 24/7 monitoring.

Are there hidden usage costs?

No. The monthly price is the price. We don't charge extra for API usage, messages sent, or any "gotcha" tier limits. Fixed pricing, no surprises.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no guilt trips, no exit interviews. Email us and you're done. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not happy.

Privacy & Security

Is my data safe?

Yes. We don't sell your data. We don't train AI on your data. Your conversations stay yours. We use encryption and industry-standard security practices.

What if the AI messes up?

It might — AI isn't perfect. That's why we have approval workflows. You can review important content before it goes out. And unlike human employees, AI learns from every correction and won't make the same mistake twice.

Capabilities

What can my AI employee actually do?

Depends on which you hire. Morgan writes content (posts, blogs, newsletters). Alex handles sales outreach and lead research. Riley manages social media scheduling and engagement. Casey handles calendar, email triage, and admin tasks.

What integrations do you support?

Currently: Telegram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook (via Metricool), Google Calendar, Gmail. Need something we don't support? Ask — we may be able to add it.

Is this a finished product?

No — we're in beta. Things are moving fast, we're improving constantly, and you're an early adopter. We're transparent about this because we respect your time and money.
